### morbidium-spritesheets

Create consistent animated 2D character sprite sheets, paper-doll art, and effect sheets for the Morbidium Three.js game, with frame validation, atlases, metadata, visual QA, and a handoff Claude can integrate. Use for requests to animate a Morbidium character, make a spritesheet/spriteark, add directional walk/attack/death frames, or prepare game-ready figure art for Claude.

[SKILL.md](skills/morbidium-spritesheets/SKILL.md) · [Hele pakken](skills/morbidium-spritesheets)

python3 scripts/find.py prop-art
python3 scripts/find.py morbidium-spritesheets
